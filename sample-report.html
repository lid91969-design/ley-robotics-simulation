A local replication of the official Unitree G1 policy in MuJoCo. Three commanded speeds, one unchanged initial state, complete run records.

Attribution: the robot model and pretrained policy are from Unitree RL GYM. I did not train this policy. This sample demonstrates deployment measurement, not a new control method or an endorsement by Unitree.

What the data says

At a 0.5 m/s command, mean body-forward speed was 0.477 m/s and forward RMSE was 0.034 m/s. The robot remained above the chosen fall thresholds for all 30 seconds.

At zero command, forward speed was small but lateral RMS was 0.077 m/s. The final horizontal displacement was approximately 0.71 m. A standing-still requirement would need a separate tolerance and test.

What it does not establish

These runs do not measure terrain robustness, recovery from pushes, physical-robot transfer, a population success rate, or the cause of the lateral motion.

No comparison to other online benchmarks is claimed: initialization, physics and measurement choices may differ.
