The engineering question

What does “works” mean
at the next handoff?

If a team's requirement is to hold position, remaining upright is only one check. The next step is to agree a position tolerance, initial conditions and a repeatable test.

Our contribution: a headless adaptation of the upstream deployment path, a fixed simulation-time horizon, command selection, body-frame measurements, JSON evidence and a readable findings report.

Test conditions and limits

Upstream commit 276801e46c5d433564f24658bac64f254b7d2d4b. MuJoCo 3.11.0 · PyTorch 2.13.0+cpu · 0.002 s physics step · 0.020 s control period.

No-fall rule: base height remains at least 0.4 m, upright cosine remains at least 0.5, and state remains finite.

One default initial state per case; no perturbations. No physical robot validation, population success rate, root-cause finding or improvement is established. No client engagement or Unitree endorsement is implied.
